# Dataset provenance and licence note

Primary planned source:

Maghdid, S., Rashid, T. A., & Askar, S. (2026). *Comprehensive Dataset Profile of In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) Clinical and Embryological Data*. Mendeley Data, Version 1. https://doi.org/10.17632/hd8yy6gzc5.1

The Version 1 record describes 5,000 IVF patient records, 20 input variables, and a binary implantation outcome (0 = failed; 1 = successful). The Version 1 landing page lists CC BY 4.0. The later Version 2 record lists CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; do not assume the licence is unchanged between versions.

This repository does not redistribute the dataset. The researcher must download the intended version from its source, verify the licence and data dictionary, and keep the file local in `data/raw/` (which is git-ignored). Record the exact downloaded filename, version, retrieval date, and checksum in the research log before analysis.

The dataset's publisher-reported preprocessing and normalization must be considered during analysis. Confirm whether raw or normalized data are being used and avoid treating preprocessed values as raw clinical measurements.
